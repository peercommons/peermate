Security
• Peermate is stricter about messages from other players: a malformed or misleading one can no longer crash the app, disturb your game or reroute it.
• Other players' names show only letters, digits and simple punctuation, so a name can't pose as a message from Peermate.
• A friend can no longer take another friend's name.

Faster matching
• When many people are looking for a game, you find an opponent sooner: players who have already paired with someone else drop out of your search right away.
