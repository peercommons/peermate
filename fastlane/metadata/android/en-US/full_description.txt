Play chess your way: against the computer, with a friend on the same phone, or online with anyone, with no server in between.

PLAY THE COMPUTER
• 10 levels, from just-learning-the-moves to full engine strength
• Play as White, Black or random
• Undo moves, and review the game with move-by-move analysis when it ends

PASS & PLAY
• Two players, one phone; the board turns for each move

PLAY ONLINE, PHONE TO PHONE
• Quick Match pairs you with someone at a similar rating
• Games run directly between the two phones. When a direct connection isn't possible, they go through public Nostr relays, still encrypted
• Bullet, blitz, rapid and classical time controls, with clocks both phones agree on
• Every move is signed, so a modified app can't rewrite the game

FRIENDS
• Add friends with a link or a QR code
• See when friends are online, and challenge them to a game
• Send private, end-to-end encrypted messages
• Challenge a friend whose app is closed: they get a notification

FAIR, VERIFIABLE RATINGS
• A Glicko-2 rating for each time control, worked out on your phone
• Both players sign every result. If you choose to publish your games, opponents can check that your rating is real

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your games and settings stay on your phone
• Your online ID is a key made on your phone; a backup phrase restores it on a new one
• Block and report players; your block list follows your ID, encrypted

Peermate is free and has no in-app purchases.
